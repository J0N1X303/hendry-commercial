How Cara thinks

Better questions. Better judgement.

Start with relevance. Use open, neutral questions to understand the customer's world. Listen before you pitch. Don't assume there's a problem. And know when a conversation isn't worth pursuing.

Closed questions have a place for clarification, permission and agreed next steps — not as the default for discovery.

Cold Call Cara is an experimental, free coaching utility from Hendry Commercial. It's not an AI imitation of a prospect; it helps you prepare for a real person.

We don't send your entered details to a server. Only your offer is optionally saved in your browser. Outcome reflections aren't stored. Avoid entering confidential or sensitive prospect information.

For UK outbound calls, check the applicable ICO telephone marketing guidance, including TPS/CTPS screening and objections.
