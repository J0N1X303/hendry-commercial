About

I sit at the point where strategy has to become commercially useful.

I’m a commercial leader with 14+ years across enterprise sales, procurement, FMCG, consulting, hospitality and business building.

Most recently I led sales and commercial performance across a global capability business, with responsibility for revenue, forecasting, budgeting, pricing, margin, enterprise growth and board reporting.

I’m particularly interested in the messy middle: where a business has an opportunity, a commercial problem or a promising idea, but needs clearer judgement, sharper execution and fewer unnecessary layers between the two.
